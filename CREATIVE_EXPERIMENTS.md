# Creative experiments

These repositories are personal game-design and software experiments, separate from my core environmental engineering and scientific-software work.

They remain public so that the code, design decisions and evolving implementations can be inspected. Public visibility does **not** imply production readiness, professional game-development experience or completion of the narrative.

## [COSMOS: The Journey of Alexander von Humboldt](https://github.com/tiagodinis90/cosmos-humboldt-rpg)

An experimental narrative game inspired by Alexander von Humboldt's expeditions and history of science. Its code explores an original walking/navigation interface, a branching narrative graph, dice-driven resolution, state changes and a historically informed content workflow.

The project also connects to my interests in environmental observation and the history of scientific measurement. Historical accuracy, creative interpretation and gameplay mechanics should be evaluated separately. The repository documents its source and intellectual-property boundaries.

## [KIRIFUSHI](https://github.com/tiagodinis90/kirifushi-rpg)

A React/TypeScript browser RPG prototype exploring branching dialogue, conditional choices, character-state transitions, skill checks and browser persistence. It is an experiment in interaction and game-state architecture, not a released game or a Godot implementation.

## How these fit the portfolio

These projects exercise software-design concepts outside the environmental domain. They are kept here for transparency and curiosity, but the [main profile](README.md) prioritizes LCA software, environmental data and reproducibility.

Both repositories can be made private later without affecting the engineering portfolio. GitHub does not provide folders for grouping independent repositories; this page is the separate index.
