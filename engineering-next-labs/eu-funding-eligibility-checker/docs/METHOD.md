# Method and limitations

A call record must be **manually transcribed and reviewed** from actual official call text. The demonstration call is invented and explicitly marked as such. Comparison uses ISO 8601 timestamps with explicit time zones and checks consortium size, declared applicant role and evidence completeness.

Results distinguish FAIL, NEEDS_REVIEW and CHECKS_PASSED_NOT_ELIGIBILITY. This is not a substitute for the call, work programme, General Annexes or legal review. No live call discovery or submission occurs.

Official context: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-and-tenders/how-apply/application-process_en
